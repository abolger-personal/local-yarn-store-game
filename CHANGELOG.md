# Changelog

What's new in L.Y.S. — Local Yarn Store. [Play it here.](https://abolger-personal.github.io/local-yarn-store-game/)

## 0.0.1 — coming soon

### The shop
- The blue Drop Off square is gone; a second table stands in its corner. Customers lend a finished
  piece at the counter when they sign the Guestbook.
- Customers' delight is easier to spot: a bigger hop for everyone, a wagging tail on lions and
  flapping ears on llamas.

### Fixes
- A selfie's caption wraps under the photo instead of stretching across the screen.
- The packing slip orders "units of yarn", not "balls": yarn also comes as skeins, cakes and cones.
- In the Guestbook, a customer who finished a project and lent it to the shop gets 🏁, not ✨; ✨ is
  only the sparkle when a customer has everything their project needs.
- You can watch a customer knit or crochet the last stretch of their project in the chair; the round that finished it used to show them sitting still.

## 0.0.0 — 2026-10-12

The first public build: a three-day tutorial in a small yarn shop.

### The shop
- Owl, llama and lion customers browse, buy, sit and knit, wear what they made, and come back as
  regulars.
- Arrange stock on shelves, tables and the floor; customers notice only what's on top, and grouped
  stock sells.
- The Guestbook records each customer's visit — what they bought, what they made, and a selfie with it.
- Hover an item or a customer to see its label: what it is, or what they bought and are making.

### The tutorial
- Day 1: the Former Owner signs the Guestbook and hands over the shop; a friend comes in for a pot
  holder kit.
- Night: order tomorrow's stock on the supplier's packing slip, with the Guestbook open beside it.
- Day 3: the friend comes back and finishes the pot holder in the shop.
