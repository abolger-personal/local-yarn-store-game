NPCs under the hood should be controlled by a few "personality" traits that will indicate how they interact with the LYS inventory and each other.  Each NPC gets a value for each numbered item from 1-5. 

Neuroticism:
1. Volatility: Tendency to react emotionally and impulsively to stress or negative events.
2. Withdrawal: Tendency to feel helpless, anxious, or depressed when facing threats or uncertainty.

Extraversion:
3. Enthusiasm: Tendency to experience positive emotions, friendliness, and joy.
4. Assertiveness: Tendency to take charge, be socially dominant, and speak up.

Openness / Intellect:
5. Intellect: Engagement with abstract ideas, logic, and problem-solving.
6. Openness: Appreciation for art, beauty, fantasy, and deep aesthetic experiences.

Agreeableness:
7. Compassion: Empathy and deep concern for the well-being of others.
8. Politeness: Tendency to respect rules, authority, and avoid aggressive conflict.

Conscientiousness:
9. Industriousness: Self-discipline, drive to complete tasks, and hard work.
10. Orderliness: Preference for organization, structure, and neatness.




Instructions for AI: 
Context: when suggesting or designing behaviors: these are based on a 10 asset break down of the Big 5 personality traits- https://deyoung.psych.umn.edu/research. 
When building, coding, and designing this game, do not use synonyms for the above 10 numbered items as they will map to a 10 point scale for each NPC. If the game designer or prompter uses a synonym, please offer them a correction or ask for clarification. 

Examples of How these are planned to be used in game (more can be added and suggested):
- Compassion, Politeness, Assertiveness, and Enthusiasm will be used to determine how to get NPCs to interact with each other
- Orderliness, Assertiveness, and Politeness will be used in the NPC-Display lower level interactions: do they mess up the player's displays? Do they help the player out by re-ordering for NPC preferences? 

- Enthusiasm and Openness and Volatility will be used to affect max NUMBER OF WIPS: the sum of these 3 is the max number of WIPs an NPC can have (15) 

- Key mechanic here is a GENERATOR MODEL that means the attributes can both generate script/guestbook entries for an NPC that sound like them but ALSO can be used to create NPCs with attributes that result in inventory preferences that the player infer. Mechanic goal is that getting to know your NPCs will help the player better solve the puzzle and generate both more Finished Objects and make NPCs stay in the store longer. 

Examples: 
- High Industriousness -> large projects that take many balls of yarn. Blankets, sweaters
- Compassion -> common charity knitting projects. prefer simple patterns, warm Hats, baby blankets. 
- Intellect -> complex patterns with high Ravelry difficulty level: Pattern level high, crochet and needle size tiny, needing many yarn colors. 
- High Volatility -> lots of WIPs, preference will be for something unlike their previous items.
