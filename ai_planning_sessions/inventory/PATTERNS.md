Patterns MVP: 
- A pattern is 3D a box like a notion, but to should be given labels on the box to make it look like a BOOK. Cover icons match pattern types. 

- MVP Pattern types: sweater (sweater icon), hat (hat with pom icon), cowl (droopy donut icon), shawl (triangle)
- Physical grid wise it is always 2x2x1. 

- Pattern non visible attributes: 
-- units of yarn required (1 to 20, as noted in WIP-AND-FINISHED-OBJ-FORMATION.md)
        - sweater takes 20 units
        - hat take 5 units
        - cowl takes 2 units
        - shawl takes 10 units
        - mittens take 6 units
    - units of yarn will map to their physical volume grid size: 1x1x1 yarn item = 1 unit yarn. 3x3x3 yarn item = 9 units. 

-- recommended minimum number of yarn colors
-- recommended material(s) 
-- recommended yarn weight(s)
-- craft type: knitting or crochet
-- a (invisible to player) list of rendering metadata hints that will turn into NPC clothing to be rendered if the NPC makes the object. 
    MVP version: ["stripes", "star", "pom pom", "fringe"]
